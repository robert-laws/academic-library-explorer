## What to measure
Inclusion is measurable through discovery outcomes, not self-reported policy statements.

### Practical metrics
- Search result diversity by discipline and creator profile.
- Representation balance in recommendations and subject groupings.
- Timeliness of inclusion-related correction requests and responses.

### Operational response
Document baseline metrics, set improvement targets, and publish quarterly progress notes to make inclusion a governed operational goal.
