## What changed
Funders and publishers are adjusting compliance requirements and contract language faster than many institutions can refresh local policy and operations.

### Current signals
- Reporting and transparency language is being tightened.
- Versioning language is increasingly linked to rights workflows.
- Libraries are expected to align local policy interpretation with external contract obligations.

### Action
- Review contract glossaries and rights language before renewal windows.
- Align repository status and deposit steps with funder checkpoints.
- Coordinate legal, collections, and technical staff on policy exceptions.
