## The risk
Contracts can fail in small windows. Missing an expiry or replacement option can create immediate access impact.

### Watchlist fields
- Contract name and expiry date.
- Annual spend, usage trend, and renewal decision owner.
- Replacement pathways and substitute access options.

### Operating rhythm
- Trigger communication 90 days ahead of each expiry.
- Link each contract to service criticality and fallback options.
- Escalate unresolved renewals through monthly leadership cycles.
