## Why run this now
Discovery quality decays without routine measurement. A recurring audit converts “cleanup work” into a governed operating process.

### Audit cadence
- Run quarterly completeness checks for identifier, abstract, language, and rights fields.
- Track duplicate suppression and unresolved link rates.
- Maintain a public remediation backlog with due dates and ownership.

### Recommended cadence
- Weekly spot checks for critical high-traffic collections.
- Monthly team-level review of recurring failure patterns.
- Quarterly board-level summary to leadership.
