## Why it matters
A weak readiness posture can turn routine technical debt into user-facing loss. Readiness needs observable checkpoints, not only archival aspirations.

### Minimum checks
- Fixity at ingest and regular scheduled re-validation.
- Documented format migration roadmap for priority collections.
- Quarterly restore simulation with logged outcomes.

### Recommended outcome
Use these checkpoints to drive staffing, budget, and vendor priorities before incidents force expensive responses.
