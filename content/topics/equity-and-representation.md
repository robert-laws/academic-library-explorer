## Why this matters now
Library services are judged by whose scholarship is visible and reusable. If metadata and discovery workflows are not inclusive, collections reproduce structural imbalance.

## Inclusion checkpoints
- Audit search facets, recommendation sets, and authority terms.
- Evaluate multilingual and cross-cultural discoverability.
- Expand community participation in description and subject interpretation.

## Operational recommendations
- Shift from occasional fairness reviews to recurring inclusion audits.
- Publish measurable representation indicators.
- Close loops between user feedback and metadata improvement cycles.

## Action checklist
- Add representation criteria to collection review routines.
- Tag and monitor correction requests with SLA targets.
- Validate search outcomes against course and department diversity metrics.
