## Why this matters now
Privacy and rights obligations are becoming part of everyday library operations. Users expect seamless service while regulators and institutions demand clear legal footing.

## Policy tensions
- Balancing personalization with privacy minimization.
- Tracking rights metadata across open, licensed, and embargoed materials.
- Training staff to interpret permissions in practical service flows.

## Practical path
- Define a unified rights model for discovery, access, and reuse.
- Keep usage analytics purposeful and limited.
- Publish plain-language notices for rights and retention expectations.

## Action checklist
- Add a data-minimization policy to discovery and analytics stacks.
- Add rights status checks to workflow gates.
- Review vendor and internal logging settings for principle-of-least-data collection.
