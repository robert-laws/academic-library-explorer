## Why this matters now
Academic libraries are central to affordability and access policy. Open access choices shape collection spending, publication visibility, and publisher negotiations.

## Current pressure points
- **APCs and budget predictability**: Institutions report volatile costs and uneven forecasting.
- **Policy interoperability**: Funder mandates are increasingly precise but implemented inconsistently.
- **Governance and trust**: Reliable licensing and rights practices define usable repository services.

## Strategic takeaways
1. Track annual APC commitments against output volume and disciplinary impact.
2. Require rights-aligned deposits and license metadata in repository workflows.
3. Use consortium coordination to balance negotiated discounts with local flexibility.

## Action checklist
- Publish a campus-level open access policy map by program and funder.
- Add a workflow for embargo and rights checks before public release.
- Review transformation agreements and APC spending quarterly.
